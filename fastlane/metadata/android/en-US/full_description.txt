DebForge downloads the files in your debrid account straight to your phone, tablet or Android TV. Sign in to one or more services and switch between them any time.

<b>Supported services</b>
<ul>
<li>TorBox</li>
<li>Real-Debrid</li>
<li>AllDebrid</li>
<li>Premiumize</li>
<li>Debrid-Link</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Fast downloads with up to 16 connections per file, pause and resume, and automatic link refresh</li>
<li>Library view with clean titles, series grouped by season, and search across your whole account</li>
<li>Posters from TMDB (add your own free API key in Settings)</li>
<li>Add magnet links, .torrent files and links, from the app or by sharing to it, and optionally download them as soon as they're ready</li>
<li>Built-in player that remembers where you stopped, a Continue watching row, subtitles from the torrent and the next episode ready to go, or play in VLC and others if you prefer</li>
<li>Player gestures (double-tap to skip, swipe for brightness, volume and seeking, hold for 2×, pinch to zoom), subtitle style, audio and subtitle languages remembered per show, sleep timer, A-B loop, screenshots, picture-in-picture and background play. Downloaded files play offline</li>
<li>Pixeldrain, Google Drive and direct file links download straight to your phone, no service needed</li>
<li>Follow a show and new episodes that reach your account download by themselves</li>
<li>Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts</li>
<li>Signed in to several services? Add to the one that already has it cached</li>
<li>Free-space check before downloading, and optional auto-delete of old downloads</li>
<li>Release-group notes, covers and sample clips are kept out of your library</li>
<li>Rules for Wi-Fi only, charging only, a nightly time window and a speed limit</li>
<li>Save to any folder, including SD cards and USB drives, with no special permission</li>
<li>Remove torrents from your service in the app, or automatically once they have downloaded</li>
<li>See whether a torrent is already cached before adding it (TorBox, Premiumize)</li>
<li>Data-usage statistics for Wi-Fi and mobile data</li>
<li>Back up and restore your settings, with optional password-encrypted sign-ins</li>
<li>Optional Plex/Jellyfin folder layout for shows and movies</li>
<li>Download a whole TorBox torrent as one zip</li>
<li>Works on phones, tablets and Android TV</li>
<li>Seven colour themes, light and dark, pure black for OLED, or your wallpaper colours</li>
<li>Available in 14 languages</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
API keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore and never included in backups. There are no analytics and no ads. If the app crashes, a report with tokens, links and file names removed is kept on your device, and it is only sent if you choose to share it.

DebForge needs an account with at least one of the services above. These are paid, proprietary services, which is why F-Droid marks the app with the "Non-Free Network Services" anti-feature.
